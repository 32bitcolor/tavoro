# Reports

> **Reports** show how work is flowing through your team — how quickly requests get a decision, how much the team finishes, whether milestones land on time, and where the hours go. They’re for **admins and managers**: open **Reports** in the sidebar (members don’t see it).

Pick a report with the **Intake**, **Delivery** and **Time** tabs, and a window with **30 days**, **90 days**, **6 months** or **12 months**. Short windows chart by week, longer ones by month. Hover or tab to a bar for its exact value, or open **Show as table** under a chart for every number at once. Reports name individual people, so treat them like any other team data.

## Intake

**Requests submitted** — New requests on your intake form in the window.

**Time to decision** — The median time from a request arriving to it being approved or rejected, for requests decided in the window.

**Approval rate** — Approved requests as a share of everything decided.

**Waiting now** — Requests still Submitted, Triaging or Needs info — listed oldest first under **Waiting for a decision**, so the ones that have sat longest are at the top.

## Delivery

**Cards completed** — Cards that reached a done status (such as **Done**) during the window. Moving a card back out of it un-completes it.

**Cycle time** — The median time from a card being created to it being done.

**Milestones on time** — Of the milestones due in the window, how many were marked complete by the end of their due day. The ones that weren’t are listed under **Milestones that slipped**.

**By person** — Each person’s completed, in-progress, open and overdue cards. A card with several assignees counts for each of them. For the cards themselves, see **Team**.

> **Faded bars.** Tavoro started recording exactly when cards are finished when Reports arrived. For work finished before then, it uses the card’s last edit as an estimate, and draws those bars faded.

## Time

The Time report is built from the **Time tracking** section of each project, so it stays empty until your team logs time.

- Hours logged per week or month, by project and by person.
- **Typical effort by deliverable type** — the median and average hours spent on finished deliverables of each type (storyboards, videos, assessments…), across all time, not just the window. Log time against a deliverable to feed it; it’s the number to reach for when estimating new work.

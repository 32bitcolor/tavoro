# Account & themes

> Two things every user controls for themselves, no admin required: your **password** and how Tavoro **looks**. This guide covers changing your own password from Account settings and switching among the built-in themes.

## Changing your password

Anyone signed in can update their own password — you don’t need an admin to do it. Head to **Settings → Account**, where the **Profile** card shows your name and email and a **Change password** form sits below.

1. Enter your **Current password**. This proves it’s really you and is required — the change is rejected if it’s wrong.
2. Type your **New password**, then repeat it in **Confirm new password**. The two must match.
3. Click **Update password**. On success the fields clear and you’ll see a confirmation. Your new password takes effect immediately.

> **Note.** Locked out and can’t sign in to reach this page? A workspace admin can set a new password for you from **Settings → Members** — an admin reset doesn’t need your current one. Once you’re back in, change it to something only you know. Changing your password signs you out on every other device.

> **Note.** Sign in with **Sign in with SSO**? Then your password lives with your company’s identity provider — change it there. A Tavoro password only matters if your workspace still allows password sign-in.

> **Tip.** Because the current password is mandatory, pick a strong, unique passphrase — the flow is built so a walk-by can’t change your password even at an unlocked screen without knowing the old one.

## Theming Tavoro

![Settings, Account: the Appearance theme swatches, the profile, and the change-password form](../../public/help-shots/account.png)
*Settings → Account.*

Tavoro ships with a big library of hand-tuned themes, and the entire interface re-colors instantly when you pick one — sidebar, cards, buttons, charts, and all. Find the theme picker in **Settings → Account**, under **Appearance** — a grid of swatches, each showing a preview of that theme’s background and accent color. Click one to apply it.

### How it behaves

**Applied instantly** — The switch is live — no save button, no reload. The whole UI re-themes the moment you select.

**Remembered per device** — Your choice is stored on the device you’re using, so this machine keeps your theme between visits. A different computer or browser has its own independent setting.

**System option** — Pick **System** and Tavoro follows your operating system’s light/dark preference instead of pinning a specific palette.

### The range on offer

Beyond **System**, there are seventeen named themes spanning clean neutrals to bold retro looks — plenty to find one that’s easy on your eyes for a long design session. A taste of what’s there:

- **Clean & neutral** — **Light**, **Dark**, **GitHub Light**, **Solarized Light**.
- **Cool & muted** — **Nord**, **Tokyo Night**, **One Dark**, **Ayu Mirage**, **Kanagawa**.
- **Warm & earthy** — **Everforest**, **Gruvbox**, **Rosé Pine**.
- **Rich & vivid** — **Dracula**, **Catppuccin Latte** and **Catppuccin Mocha**, **Monokai**, **Synthwave**.

> **Tip.** Every theme is designed as a complete, accessible set, so text stays readable and status colors keep their meaning no matter which you land on. Try a few — since the choice only affects your own device, you can experiment freely without touching anyone else’s view.
